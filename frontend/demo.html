What is VIT?
A full-stack portfolio management system built for a Taiwan family office — replacing manual Excel workflows with a live, multi-entity investment tracker.
Vincera Capital manages equity positions across 4 entities (two principals, two private banking arms) and 3 brokers. Before VIT, all reconciliation was done manually in Excel with no audit trail and no automated P&L tracking. VIT centralises every workflow — from trade upload to dividend management to Excel reporting — in a single web application.

📄 Trade Upload — Two Paths

PDF auto-parse: Sophie drags a broker statement PDF onto the upload zone. VIT's custom parser extracts all trades — ticker, shares, price, fee, tax, net amount — and previews them before saving. Three different broker formats (Sinopac, Mega, Cathay) are each handled by a dedicated parser accounting for their column layouts and merged-cell quirks.

Manual entry: For OTC trades or capital increases not confirmed by a broker, Sophie enters the trade directly. The form auto-calculates broker fees by account and flags net amount discrepancies before submission.

📊 Real-Time Market Prices

A single button fetches the latest closing price for every held security from the Taiwan Stock Exchange (TWSE) public API. Prices are stored and used to compute unrealized P&L across all positions instantly.

Holdings also support two price modes per security: transaction price (last trade) and average price (calculated from recent TWSE data) — toggled per stock so Sophie can compare both views without re-fetching.

⚖️ Position & P&L Calculation Engine

Every time a trade is confirmed, VIT replays the full transaction history for that entity using the weighted-average cost method to recompute cost basis and realized P&L. This guarantees accuracy even after partial sells, stock splits, or bonus share allocations.

The Holdings tab shows projected average cost when pending stock dividends exist — so Sophie sees the post-allocation price before the shares officially arrive.

🎁 Dividend Management

Cash dividends are entered on announcement date and marked "deposited" once credited — flowing into Realized P&L automatically.

Stock dividends (bonus shares) follow the same two-step pattern: pending until allocation date, with an ⏳ badge and projected new average cost shown in Holdings. One click confirms and updates the position.

Capital increases (direct cash subscriptions to unlisted companies) are also tracked — recorded on payment date, pending until shares arrive.

💰 Cash Account Ledger

VIT tracks 12 cash accounts across 4 entities — brokerage, private banking, personal bank accounts, and money market funds. Each trade automatically debits or credits the correct account.

Sophie can also record: fund transfers, bank loans, inter-entity shareholder loans (RC ↔ HuaQiang), loan repayments, and unlisted company dividend income. The Cash Accounts tab shows a full running ledger with balance history per account.

📈 Realized P&L — Full Audit Trail

The Realized P&L tab shows every income event — stock sales, money market income, and cash dividends — with computed net proceeds, cost basis, and gain/loss, filterable by entity, broker, category, and month.

Sophie can apply manual adjustments to any computed row (for rounding corrections or missing data), with the original value preserved for reference. Adjustments flow through automatically to the Holdings dashboard so both views always agree. Full CSV export with one click.

📤 Excel Export — Daily Report

The Holdings tab exports a formatted Excel report with today's date, a summary of the day's trades, full position table by entity and broker, P&L summary (realized + unrealized side-by-side), and cash balances — including private banking loan and shareholder loan balances.

Column widths, fonts, borders, and number formats match the family office's existing reporting template exactly, so the file is ready to send without further formatting.

🏗️ Architecture

Backend: Python / Flask · PostgreSQL · SQLAlchemy ORM · Railway
Frontend: Vanilla JS single-page app (no framework)
PDF parsing: Custom parsers per broker format (pdfplumber)
Excel export: openpyxl with programmatic formatting
Market data: TWSE public REST API
Auth: HTTP Basic Auth gating (demo excluded)

~4,500 lines across 6 core files. Built and iterated over 4 months while managing the family office as EVP.
